Cookie Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This site does not use cookies. Nothing here reads or writes a cookie on your device, for tracking, advertising or any other purpose.

What this site does store

One piece of local, session-only storage: a flag remembering whether the first-load intro animation has already played during your current browser session. It holds no personal data, isn’t shared with anyone, and clears itself when you close the tab. It isn’t a cookie — it’s sessionStorage, which browsers exempt from cookie-consent requirements because it’s strictly functional.
