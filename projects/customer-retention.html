Customer Retention Analytics

Data analytics · Analytics project

Understand churn and strengthen retention.

The project

A customer intelligence analysis of subscriber churn, customer lifetime value, support friction and contract commitment for a subscription platform.

Approach

The project extracts customer, subscription and support records from SQLite, cleans and joins them in Python, engineers tenure and churn features, and communicates results in an executive report.

Tools & materials

  • Python
  • Pandas
  • SQLite
  • SQL
  • Data visualization

Current stage

The documented cohort contains 21 subscribers. Its reported correlations and churn patterns should be read in that limited dataset context.
